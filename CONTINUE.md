# Sabores Mexican Cuisine: Website and Digital Growth (continuation handoff)

## 1. What this project is and why it exists

Sabores Mexican Cuisine is a restaurant in Calgary with a site at sabores.ca. Anthony met the owner onsite and came away with growth ideas. The project exists to turn that goodwill into a concrete owner-facing proposal: fix the website so it stops hurting the business, and line up the digital footprint so the restaurant's strong reputation actually converts into orders and visits.

## 2. State of the work so far

Two independent reviews are done. Aside (Sol) and Hermes each looked at the website on their own, and both converged on the same findings:

- The food reputation is strong. TripAdvisor sits at 4.6/5 and SkipTheDishes already has the full menu live.
- The website is an untouched GoDaddy template doing active harm. The meta description literally reads 'Check out this GoDaddy hosted webpage!', the Our Food page is dead, and there is no menu, no order link, no phone number, and no map anywhere on the site. The page even carries MySpace and StumbleUpon share buttons.
- The directory footprint is fragmented. There are two Instagram accounts, and conflicting phone numbers and hours across listings.

The joint review is written to the Obsidian note (path below). Nothing has been sent to the owner yet.
