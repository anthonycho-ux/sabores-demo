Subject: A small idea for Sabores, from a ten-year regular

Hi [her name],

I came by the kitchen in Cedarbrae the other day, and we talked a little. I did not say this well while I was there, but I should have. I have been eating your food for almost ten years, since the old farmers market on Macleod Trail. I found you there by luck, and I have been a regular ever since. The salsas and the tamales are the best I have found in Calgary, and the tres leches is worth the drive by itself.

That day, I told you why I was looking for a restaurant like this. I look for people who bring passion, skill, pride, and discipline to the craft of making beautiful food. That is what I found at your counter, and it is rare. I wanted you to know that.

While I was there, I also noticed the shelf inside the kitchen with the tres leches and the jars, the things you make fresh. It is the best part of your business, and it is invisible to anyone who is not standing in front of it.

I have an idea I would love to show you, with no cost and no obligation. It is a simple page, one you look at on your phone, that shows what is actually on your shelf today. A customer checks it in the morning, sees the tres leches is out and the salsa verde is fresh, and knows it is worth the trip. When something is gone, it shows gone. When the kitchen is closed, it tells them when you are back. The whole thing runs on one photo a day, the same photo you probably already take for your Instagram.

Why I care: food made with that kind of pride deserves more customers than it gets. The people who would love your food are not all walking past, but this gives them a reason to come find you.

If that sounds interesting, I would be glad to come by and show you the idea in person, five minutes, whenever suits you. If it does not sound like you, no problem at all, and I will see you at the counter either way.

Warm regards,
Anthony
